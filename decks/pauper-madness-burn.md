## How the engine works

- Discard outlets (Faithless Looting, Highway Robbery, Grab the Prize, Blood tokens from Voldaren Epicure)
  → madness **Fiery Temper** for {R}
  → **Sneaky Snacker** hits graveyard, returns for free when you draw your 3rd card in a turn
    (Faithless Looting draws 2; any prior draw step / Epicure trigger completes the count)
- Every noncreature spell pings with **Kessig Flamebreather**
- Every instant/sorcery pings with **Guttersnipe** (when in play)
- **Lava Dart** flashback = cheap spell + sacrifice (relevant for Kessig count)
- **Grab the Prize** deals 2 to each opponent when you discard a nonland — hits for 4 total (2 from spell + Kessig)
- **Fireblast** is usually the finisher — sacrifice 2 Mountains, 4 damage, free
